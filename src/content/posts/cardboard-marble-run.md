I built a marble run out of old delivery boxes. It is taller than my sister.

## The plan

I drew it first on paper. The marble starts at the top, goes round a spiral,
through a tunnel, over a jump, and lands in a cup.

## What went wrong

- **Day 1:** the spiral was too steep and the marble flew off the track
- **Day 2:** the tunnel collapsed because I used one layer of cardboard
- **Day 3:** fixed both, and the jump worked on the *first* try 🎉

## The trick I found

If you fold cardboard into a **triangle** shape, it gets way stronger than a
flat piece. Triangles are the strongest shape. I tested it by putting books on top.
