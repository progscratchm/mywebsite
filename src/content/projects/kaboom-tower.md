Stack blocks as high as you can. Then watch it all come down.

## How it works

Each block falls from the top and you tap to drop it. The physics engine does
the rest — if your tower is wobbly, it **will** fall over.

## What I had to figure out

- How to make blocks bump into each other instead of going through
- How to count the score when the tower is still moving
- How to make the kaboom sound feel satisfying (this took ages)

It's a real app on the App Store now. 🎉
